What Are Data Structures & algorithms
-------------------------------------
- Algorithm is simply a sequence of steps (i.e. solution)
    - For a given input to get the desired output


- Data structure is a piece of data w/c 
    - Follows certain rules to solve a certain problem
    - e.g. array, maps, object, etc.


- Why use them?
    - When working w/ complex problems may require the following to solve it
        - Sophisticated data storage (i.e. data structure)
        - Sophisticated computation logic (i.e. algorithm)

    - To express a solution to a problem logically as a programmer
        - To derive solutions & compare against other ones
    
    - To ace job interviews & get hired
